CANVASLINK

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026.

Pre-launch draft. CanvasLink is not yet deployed as a public bot. These pages describe the current implementation and proposed service terms. Production hosting and retention arrangements will be finalized before public launch.

1. Who operates CanvasLink

CanvasLink is operated by Ke Mi in Singapore (“we”, “us”). For privacy, support, access, correction, or deletion requests, email dulie.business@gmail.com and mention CanvasLink. This policy covers the CanvasLink website and Telegram bot; Dulie is a separate bot with its own policy.

2. Information we process

  • Telegram: user and private-chat identifiers, username, commands, and messages you send to the bot. We process messages to carry out your requests; the application stores structured records needed for its features, rather than a general conversation transcript.
  • Canvas: your private calendar feed URL and the calendar entries it returns, including titles, descriptions, course identifiers, links, dates, and cancellation information. The feed URL acts like a secret that grants access to the feed.
  • Planner and preferences: personal tasks, completion status, personal targets, snoozes, timezone, course/type settings, reminder and agenda preferences, and onboarding progress.
  • Google connection: access and refresh tokens, permission scopes, expiry, calendar identifiers and properties, calendar-list information used for destination selection, and responses about events the bot manages.
  • Operations: pending actions, calendar jobs, notification content and delivery records, connection health, temporary authorization state, and error diagnostics. Support correspondence and infrastructure access logs may contain additional technical information such as IP addresses and request times.

We do not ask for your Canvas or Google password. Google is optional. The current integration requests no Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or Google Tasks permissions.

3. Why we use information

We use this information to read your Canvas deadlines, show your planner, apply settings, send reminders and optional agendas, notify you of deadline or connection changes, and perform the Google Calendar sync you enable. Background processing continues while your connections and settings permit it. We also use necessary information to handle support, maintain security, and meet legal obligations.

CanvasLink does not use AI models to interpret your messages or process your calendar data. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

4. Optional Google Calendar access

The authorization screen describes permissions whose technical reach is broader than the events CanvasLink intends to manage:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events lets the app view and edit calendar events. CanvasLink creates, reads, updates, and removes tracked events as needed for sync and verified reset.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created permits creation of secondary calendars and management of events on calendars created by the app. CanvasLink uses this to provide a dedicated CanvasLink calendar.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.calendarlist.readonly lets CanvasLink list your calendars so you can select a writable destination.

Relevant assignment titles, descriptions, links, dates, times, and appearance settings are sent to Google. We keep event identifiers and ownership records for subsequent updates, retries, and safe deletion. The bot does not import your unrelated Google events into the Telegram planner. Selecting a shared calendar may make synced details visible to people who have access to that calendar.

Tokens allow authorized background sync without repeated sign-in. You may use /disconnect_google and remove CanvasLink in Google Account connections to stop future authorized access. See the deletion section for what remains.

Google data and Limited Use

CanvasLink’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide or improve the visible features you authorize, never for advertising, sale, credit decisions, or training general-purpose AI models.

Transfers of Google data are limited to authorized user-facing features with your consent, security purposes, legal obligations, or a business transfer with your prior explicit consent. Human access is limited to your affirmative permission for specific data, necessary security or legal purposes, or permitted aggregated internal operations. These restrictions also cover derived data and people acting on our behalf.

5. Services that receive information

  • Telegram delivers your messages and receives bot replies, deadline details, reminders, and agendas.
  • Your Canvas provider receives requests for the feed you connect; the feed contents are processed by CanvasLink.
  • Google handles OAuth and the calendar operations you authorize. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the network information needed to serve those fonts.
  • Infrastructure providers serving the website, bot, and PostgreSQL database process the information necessary to operate those components. The repository’s website deployment uses GitHub Pages. The database is hosted on Google Cloud SQL in Singapore. Production bot hosting and log/backup retention arrangements have not yet been finalized. We will update those details before launch.

These services may process information outside Singapore. We handle transfers under applicable data-protection requirements. Necessary disclosures for security or legal obligations remain subject to the stricter Google data limits above.

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

When webhook delivery is enabled, Telegram update IDs and receipt times are stored to prevent repeated commands. They contain no message body and are removed after seven days by the next successful scheduled cleanup.

CanvasLink stores account, planner, connection, and operational records in PostgreSQL. Feed URLs and Google credentials are encrypted by the application before storage. This does not mean every task or database field is encrypted by the application, or that the service provides end-to-end encrypted storage.

Account data and planner history have no general automatic age-based deletion. They remain until removed by the relevant feature or a deletion request. The scheduler prunes notification-outbox records whose expiry is more than 30 days in the past; this is not a 30-day limit for all data. Expiry of an authorization or temporary input does not necessarily mean immediate physical erasure.

  • Done: marks work complete locally and stops its reminders; it does not erase the item or submit work to Canvas.
  • /disconnect_canvas: removes the feed, course settings, and feed-backed planner items. Personal tasks, preferences, existing Google events, and ownership records needed for a later verified wipe remain.
  • /disconnect_google: removes stored Google authorization and attempts provider revocation. It retains existing Google events and local planner data.
  • /reset: after confirmation, attempts to delete verified CanvasLink-owned Google events, then removes local account and planner data. If verification or deletion fails, reset can stop with local data retained. It does not promise to delete the Google calendar itself.

For local data deletion without a Google-event wipe, or if reset cannot finish, email dulie.business@gmail.com. Include your Telegram username or identifier; never send your private feed URL, passwords, or OAuth tokens. We may verify account ownership before acting. We will explain any information that must be retained and consider logs and backups when handling the request. No fixed backup-erasure period is promised here; applicable limits will be explained in the response.

Blocking the bot or deleting the Telegram chat does not erase the database or revoke Google access. Copies independently held by Telegram, Google, or your institution are controlled through those services. Revoke or regenerate a Canvas feed through your institution if its URL has been exposed.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

Change reminder, agenda, sync, and timezone preferences in the bot. You can use the planner without connecting Google, disconnect either integration, or stop using the service. Contact the operator for access, correction, a copy, deletion, or withdrawal of consent. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing and complain to a privacy regulator. We respond within applicable legal requirements and explain lawful limitations.

We retain information only while needed for the disclosed service, security, or legal purposes. Where a legal basis is required, processing relies on providing the service you request, consent where required, permitted service/security interests, or legal obligations. Google data remains subject to the narrower limits above.

8. Website and security

The website stores your light/dark theme preference in browser local storage. Its interactive previews use sample data and do not access your accounts. It does not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or application cookies. Hosting and font providers may receive technical request information. External links lead to services with their own policies.

The bot attempts to delete the Telegram message containing your feed URL after you submit it; if that fails, it asks you to delete it. Do not share the URL publicly. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

9. Updates

We will update this policy when our practices change and provide notice of material changes through the service where appropriate. New uses requiring consent will be presented before that processing begins. The date above identifies this version.
