2. What CanvasLink does

CanvasLink reads a Canvas calendar feed, provides a Telegram planner and reminders, and optionally syncs deadlines to Google Calendar. Available features depend on the deployed version and your settings. Website previews contain sample data, not your actual assignments.

Canvas and your institution remain the source for official deadlines and submission requirements. Feed updates, parsing, background sync, and messages can be delayed, incomplete, duplicated, or unavailable. Check important dates in Canvas and maintain another reminder method for critical work.

Marking work Done only changes CanvasLink’s local planner; it does not submit an assignment. Personal targets change reminder timing, not official deadlines. Personal tasks stay in Telegram and are not automatically synced to Google Calendar.
